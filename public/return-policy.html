Return and Refund Policy

This policy applies to orders placed at jamebouquets.in.

Change-of-mind returns

We do not accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind on correctly supplied items, including handmade and customised bouquets and gifts.

Problems with your order

If your item arrives damaged or defective, is incorrect, differs from what was advertised or agreed, or arrives after the stated delivery schedule, contact us for help. We will arrange any return, refund or other remedy you are entitled to under applicable consumer law.

How to get help

Email contactjamebouquets@gmail.com with your order ID and a description of the issue. If available, attach photos of the item and packaging. We’ll explain the next steps, including how to return the item if needed.

Your statutory consumer rights apply.
